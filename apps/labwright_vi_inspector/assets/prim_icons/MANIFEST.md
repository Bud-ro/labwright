# Primitive icon assets

Generated from LabVIEW's own renders in the snippet corpus (see
tool/extract_prim_icons.dart): per identity, the hard-edged samples
(every colour in the node box tiles at least three pixels; renders
that anti-alias the icon are set aside) are aligned and
consensus-voted per pixel (attached wires and neighbour ink vanish
where the samples disagree), edge-touching wire stubs are erased, the
result is trimmed to its ink and the exterior background made
transparent. Colours are the canonical ones of the inspector's colour
aliases. An identity with no hard-edged render is cut from its
anti-aliased ones and says so; where its silhouette is one the
hard-edged icons share, its border is theirs and the row says which.
Hand-edits welcome — the painter stamps these at natural size.
